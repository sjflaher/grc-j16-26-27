The Truth About Where We Start

  • The clubs we have to beat:
  • Have more rowers than us
  • Have everyone in one school
  • Get more mid-week water time
They can afford wasted sessions. We can't.
And that discipline — never wasting a session — is exactly how we beat them.

Laying Bricks

  • Every session = one brick in a wall
  • ~400 hours this season ≈ 306 bricks
  • 400 hours makes us competitive
  • Winning means alomst every brick is STONE, not cardboard

A stone brick =

  • 1. Prepped — rested, fed, hydrated
  • 2. Full effort — physical and mental, hit your target
  • 3. Recovered — sleep and food are the mortar. A great session with no recovery is a brick with no mortar.

Good training doesn't just happen in the hour you're in the club

1. Prep

  • This is YOUR job, not your parents'
  • Can't make training? YOU message me, in good time. Not your mum. Not your dad.
  • Your food, your water bottle, your sleep, your kit
  • Know your targets before you walk in the door
Arrive rested, fed, hydrated — ready to work.

2. Training

A target for EVERY session.

🏋️
Gym (S&C)
  • Know Weights / reps to hit
  • Progressive overload
  • Warm-up, lock-in, work hard
🏃‍♂️
Erg & Cardio
  • Wattage targets for every erg session
  • Aiming to beat last weeks time on every run
🚣
Water
  • Down 30 mins before session, hands on 15 before
  • 1 personal technique focus
  • 1 crew focus
  • Boat speed targets
You should never sit on an erg or get in a boat
without knowing exactly what you're trying to do.

3. Recovery — The Mortar

  • A great session with no recovery is a brick with no mortar
  • Never be hungry
  • Protein ~2g per kg bodyweight · carbs AND fats matter
  • Fruit & veg — Eat as much as you can
  • 7–9 hours sleep · consistent times · wind-down routine
  • HR monitor → resting HR & HRV tell us when to push and when to back off
Sleep and food are the mortar that holds the wall together.
