The Headwinds we face

  • At GRC we face structural disadvantages against the biggest clubs:
  • Smaller athlete pool
  • Boys spread across different schools, some out of town
  • Limited scope for mid-week water sessions
To win, we need to turn these things into an advantage
  • Hyper-focused and individualised training
  • Robust, flexible training plans
  • Athlete driven momentum

What We Need From Parents

  • Support the standards at home — sleep, food, hydration (details with the boys)

What The Boys Own

  • This year, responsibility sits with the athletes:
  • Attendance & communication — if a boy can't make a session, he tells the coach in good time, not a parent
  • Nutrition, hydration, sleep, session prep and recovery
  • Personal targets for every single session
  • Block goals, set 1:1 with the coach
Parents get the schedule and the race calendar.
The boys get everything else.

Tracking, Targets & Technology

  • Individual targets replace crew averages this year
  • Every boy has personal erg, strength and technique targets per block — and a defined target for every session
  • Data captured via Lumin app + heart-rate monitors (Polar)
  • New this season: a squad web portal — boys log results, see personal targets and progression (in development)
  • Close monitoring = early, supportive intervention where needed. Progress is the motivator.
