## Slide 5 — Laying Bricks
Think of the season as building a wall. Every session = one brick.

- ~400 hours this season ≈ **306 bricks**
- 400 hours of training makes us *competitive*
- **Winning means every brick is stone, not cardboard**

A stone brick =
1. Turn up **prepped** — rested, fed, hydrated
2. **Full effort** — physical AND mental, hit your target
3. **Recover properly** — sleep and food are the *mortar*. A great session with no
   recovery is a brick with no mortar.

*Notes: origin story — grain of rice in a jar, and its flaw: the rice drops in
whatever the quality. Bricks are different: quality is the whole point.*

---

## Slide 6 — Three Questions After Every Session
1. Did I arrive prepped?
2. Did I hit my target?
3. Will I recover properly?

**3 yeses = a stone brick in the wall.**

## Slide 7 — The Season: 5 Blocks
Block 1 (Sep-Oct) → Block 2 (Nov-Dec) → Block 3 (Jan-Mar) → Block 4 (Mar-May) → Block 5 (May-**CHAMPS**)

At the **start** of every block, each of you meets me 1:1:
- Set your goals for the block
- Sort your schedule — then **your availability is locked in**
- Agree any personal tweaks to the programme

At the **end** of every block: testing + review. You'll see exactly what the block built.

**Missing a session you committed to should be extremely rare.** Life happens —
but "committed" means committed.

---

## Slide 8 — Your Targets, Not Crew Averages
This year there are no crew averages. **Every one of you gets personal targets:**
- **Erg**: wattage targets, per block, per session
- **S&C**: weight/rep progressions
- **Technique**: specific refinements to make each block

And **every single session has a target**:
| Session | Your target |
|---|---|
| Erg | A wattage to hold |
| S&C | Weights/reps to hit |
| Water | 1 personal technique focus + 1 crew focus + boat speed |

You should never sit on an erg or get in a boat without knowing exactly what
you're trying to do.

*Notes: targets derived from your own test results — they move when you improve.
Progress against your own numbers is the scoreboard.*

## Slide 10 — The Week (winter shape)
- Tue: **Morning erg** + evening S&C
- Wed: Run
- Thu: **Morning erg** + evening S&C

championship level. National-level juniors train 600+ hrs/year; we're moving
from ~305 to ~400, and making every hour count double.
