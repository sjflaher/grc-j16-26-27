## Slide 5 — The Honest Starting Point (Fighting Headwinds)
At GRC we face structural headwinds vs the biggest clubs:
- Smaller athlete pool
- Boys spread across different schools, some out of town
- Limited scope for mid-week water sessions

**Our answer: we will not out-volume anyone — we will out-think, out-prepare and
out-execute them.** Every session smart, efficient and intentional.

*Notes: this is also why the parent logistics piece matters — the sessions we CAN
do together have to happen and have to count.*

## Slide 7 — British Rowing: Rower Pathway
| Level | Description | Age | Sessions/wk | Session Length | Hours/wk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Level 4** | Competitive club / High Performance | 17+ | **6–8** | Water <90’ / Land <80’ | **6–12** |
| ↑ **GRC J16: 7–10 sessions/wk · ~9 hrs/wk** ↑ |||||
| **Level 3** | National events | 15+ | **4–6** | Water <75’ / Land <60’ | **4–8** |
| Level 2 | Developing / local events | 13+ | 3–4 | 45–60’ | 2–4 |
| Level 1 | All rowers (skill development) | 11+ | — | 30–40’ | 1–2 |

- GRC J16 exceeds Level 3 (national) and overlaps into Level 4 (high performance)
- Training at 15–16 like competitive 17+ rowers — building toward senior success

*Notes: GRC J16 sits at the TRANSITION between Level 3 and Level 4 — exceeding national-level volume, approaching high-performance territory.*

## Slide 13 — What The Boys Own (Athlete-Led Standards)
This year, responsibility sits with the athletes:
- **Attendance & communication**: if a boy can't make a session, *he* tells the coach,
  in good time — not a parent
- Nutrition, hydration, sleep, session prep and recovery
- Personal targets for every single session
- Block goals, set 1:1 with the coach

**Parents get the schedule and the race calendar. The boys get everything else.**

*Notes: frame positively — this is athlete development, not parent exclusion.
It's also what senior/college programmes will expect of them.*

## Slide 14 — Tracking, Targets & Technology
- **Individual targets replace crew averages this year** — every boy has personal
  erg, strength and technique targets per block
- Every session has a defined target for every athlete
- Data captured via Lumin app + heart-rate monitors (Polar)
- New this season: a squad web portal where boys log results and see their
  personal targets and progression *(in development)*
- Close monitoring means early, supportive intervention if someone is struggling —
  progress is the motivator, not punishment
