1. CONSISTENT LANE BIAS:

   - Apply the overall bias adjustment for that lane
   
2. PERIOD-SPECIFIC BIAS:
   - If a lane is only biased in one period → likely wind/current at that time
   - Apply period-specific adjustment for races in that period only
   
3. STATISTICAL SIGNIFICANCE:
   - Only apply adjustments when ANOVA shows significance
   - For borderline cases, present both adjusted and unadjusted rankings
