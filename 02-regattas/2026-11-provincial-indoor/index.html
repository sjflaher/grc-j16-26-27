2k targets and race plan. All splits are /500m on the monitor.

Race plan

Same plan for everyone. Split the 2k into four 500s:

  1. First 500 — target split minus 1 secondGet out quickly, then settle. Don’t go faster than this.
  2. Second 500 — at target splitSettle onto your number. Long and controlled.
  3. Third 500 — at target splitHold it. This is the one that decides the race.
  4. Last 500 — your callRow whatever split you feel. The number on your card is the slowest you can go and still hit your 2k target. Anything faster is a bonus.
Why it works: 1 second banked in the first 500 + 2 × 500s on target means you can afford target + 1 second in the last 500 and still finish on your 2k target.
