# GRC Mens J16 2026/27 — Season Principles

## 1. Fighting Headwinds
GRC has structural disadvantages vs top competition: smaller athlete pool, athletes
spread across schools and out of town, limited mid-week water access. We cannot
out-volume the big clubs on the water — so we must be **smarter, more efficient and
more intentional** in every session than anyone we race. Efficiency is our edge.

## 2. Athlete-Led Standards
Goal last year; not achieved. This year it's non-negotiable.
- **Parents get**: the training schedule and race calendar (logistics for morning
  sessions etc. fall on them).
- **Athletes own everything else**: attendance, nutrition, recovery, session prep.
- If an athlete can't make training, **he** tells the coach in good time — not a parent.

## 3. Laying Bricks (core season analogy)
Each session = a brick in a wall. (Evolution of the "grain of rice in a jar" idea —
flaw: the rice goes in regardless of session quality.)
- ~400 hrs of training makes us competitive; **winning requires every brick to be stone,
  not cardboard**.
- A quality brick = high physical AND mental effort in the session.
- Turning up properly prepped (rested, fed, hydrated) is part of the brick.
- Recovery (sleep, nutrition) is the **mortar** — a good session without recovery is
  a brick with no mortar.

## 4. Block Structure
Season = 5 blocks. At the **start** of each block:
- Training programme set out in minute detail
- 1:1 meeting with each athlete: block goals set, schedules accounted for,
  personal amendments to the general plan made
- **Availability locked in** for the block

At the **end** of each block: testing period + review.

Missing a committed session should be an extreme occurrence (life happens, but
extenuating circumstances only).

## 5. Personal Goals & Targets
Every athlete has per-block progression targets:
- Erg wattage
- Strength progression
- Technical refinements

## 6. A Target for Every Session
The operational expression of "laying bricks" — every athlete has a defined target
for every session:
- **Erg**: wattage target
- **S&C**: weight/reps target
- **Water**: one personal technique target + one crew technique target + boat speed targets
